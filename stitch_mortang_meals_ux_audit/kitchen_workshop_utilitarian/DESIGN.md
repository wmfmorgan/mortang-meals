## Brand & Style

This design system reimagines everyday domestic kitchen planning through the lens of an experimental, utilitarian recipe workshop. It discards precious domestic gentility in favor of industrial efficiency, strict discipline, and architectural clarity. The experience treats food preparation as a rigorous craft: recipes are production manifests, meal plans are operational matrices, and shopping lists are itemized raw-material inventories.

The aesthetic fuses stark utilitarian brutalism with the tactile warmth of culinary terracotta. Heavy structural gridlines, unapologetic 90-degree squared corners, monospaced metadata densities, and severe typographic contrasts create an environment calibrated for focus, high-speed execution, and unvarnished honesty. The interface evokes the atmosphere of a professional test kitchen or an architect's prep station: functional, structured, tactile, and completely free of superfluous decoration.

## Colors

The color palette anchors on raw architectural materials, organic culinary dyes, and high-legibility contrasting ink:

- **Primary (`#8a3b2a`)**: A rich, grounded kiln-fired terracotta rust that drives urgent status markers, production alerts, primary focal actions, and mechanical highlights.
- **Secondary (`#5c6b3a`)**: A culinary olive tone utilized for operational confirmation, status affirmations, ratings, and botanical categorizations.
- **Tertiary (`#7a7265`)**: An herb taupe configured for secondary metrics, time indicators, tool specifications, and passive labels.
- **Neutral (`#2c2416`)**: Deep umber ink serving as structural boundaries, stark framing lines, and high-contrast typographic content.

### Backgrounds & Tactical Washes
The canvas sits on unbleached linen (`#f4efe6`), providing a soft, non-reflective base. Elevated workshops and panels utilize parchment paper (`#fff9f1`). Meal categories receive specific contextual fills within the matrix: Breakfast (`#fbf7ef`), Lunch (`#f7f3ea`), and Dinner (`#f3efe2`). Structural division is maintained via hairline wheat (`#e4d9c8`) and draft dashes (`#c9bda8`).

## Typography

The typographical framework contrasts the aggressive, technical geometry of `Space Grotesk` against the surgical, high-density legibility of `Geist`. 

- **Headlines & Structural Anchors (`Space Grotesk`)**: Employed for primary display banners, operational stage headers, and meal card titles. Its idiosyncratic mechanical curves provide an authoritative, utilitarian workshop personality.
- **Body & Data Layer (`Geist`)**: Used for all instructions, ingredient lists, quantities, cooking procedures, and metadata badges.
- **Data Densities & System Eyebrows**: Formatted in full uppercase with expanded letter-spacing (`0.08em` - `0.14em`) to mirror shop blueprints, industrial labeling systems, and laboratory manifests.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is treated as a structural grid frame inspired by industrial prep schedules and modular assembly lines. 

- **Grid Architecture**:
  - **Desktop (1024px+)**: A strict 7-column planner matrix representing the week, spanning up to a maximum constrained width of `1280px` (`margin: 0 auto`). Column gutters sit tightly at `0.75rem` to enforce high information density and structural coherence.
  - **Tablet (640px – 1023px)**: Reflows into a 2-column operational layout; planning matrices transition into grouped day-batches.
  - **Mobile (< 640px)**: Collapses into an authoritative vertical stack. Outer margins clamp down to `1rem` to maximize touch target efficiency and reading area.
- **Rhythm**: All paddings and layout increments strictly follow an 8px grid with 4px sub-increments (`space-xs` to `space-xl`). Components prioritize functional compactness over airy decorative white space.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system completely rejects soft, blurred, ambient skeuomorphic drop-shadows and frosted glass treatments. Depth is articulated through **structural layering, hard offsets, and high-contrast bounding lines**:

- **Hard Surface Boundaries**: Tiers are demarcated via crisp `1px` or `2px` solid borders using Umber Ink (`#2c2416`) or Wheat Hairlines (`#e4d9c8`).
- **Mechanical Offsets (Hard Shadows)**: High-priority interactive elements or active flyouts project flat, hard-edged shadow planes with zero blur radius: `2px 2px 0px #2c2416` or `4px 4px 0px #2c2416`.
- **Drawer Panels & Modals**: Layer directly over a dimming background wash of translucent ink (`rgba(44, 36, 22, 0.45)`) and terminate in a stark, full-height mechanical boundary.
- **Empty & Draft States**: Visualized using `1px dashed #c9bda8` perimeters, signaling operational slots waiting for inventory or action.

## Shapes

The design system is rigorously sharp. Every border radius across all controls, cards, badges, dialogs, and inputs is locked to `0px`. 

Corners are razor-sharp right angles, referencing technical blueprints, modular kitchen prep tables, and brutalist print layouts. The refusal of rounded corners strips away consumer app softness, directing user attention strictly to operational throughput, meal data, and step-by-step methodology.

## Components

### Buttons & Operational Triggers
- **Primary Button**: Solid terracotta background (`#8a3b2a`), stark paper text (`#fff9f1`), square profile (`roundedness: 0`), framed with a `1px solid #2c2416` outline. On hover, shifts to deep terracotta (`#6b2c1f`) with a `2px 2px 0px #2c2416` hard offset.
- **Secondary Button**: Crisp parchment fill (`#fff9f1`), `1px solid #2c2416` border, umber typography (`#2c2416`). Hover switches border to olive (`#5c6b3a`) with immediate color inversion.
- **Empty Cell Button (`+ ADD`)**: Monospaced uppercase text, transparent backdrop, framed by a `1px dashed #c9bda8` border. Hover turns the container into an inverse solid wash.

### Cards & Meal Containers
- **Meal Cards**: Squared, multi-layer cards resting on slot-specific background washes (Breakfast `#fbf7ef`, Lunch `#f7f3ea`, Dinner `#f3efe2`). Enclosed in `1px solid #e4d9c8`. Active state features a `2px 2px 0px #2c2416` hard drop.
- **Card Headers**: Employs uppercase category metadata labels paired with bold `Space Grotesk` titles.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Text Inputs**: Crisp square rectangular fields, `1px solid #e4d9c8`, `min-height: 44px`, background `#fff9f1`. Focus state snaps instantly to `2px solid #8a3b2a` with no fuzzy glow.
- **Input Labels**: Compact, uppercase tracking (`Geist`, `0.75rem`, `letterSpacing: 0.08em`).

### Checkboxes & Quantitative Lists
- **Checkboxes**: Hard-edged `16px x 16px` boxes, `1.5px solid #2c2416`, background `#fff9f1`. When checked, fills solid terracotta (`#8a3b2a`) with a square white glyph; the associated item text renders with a clean strike-through.
- **Aisle Headers**: Utilitarian monospaced inventory banners spanning full container widths with a bottom divider rule.

### Status Chips & Metadata Badges
- **Badges**: Compact, boxy tags with `0px` radius, `1px solid #2c2416`, background `#f4efe6`, text in `Geist` uppercase.
- **Timing / Servings Indicators**: Monospaced numerical units separated by sharp vertical dividers (`|`).

### Drawers & Slide-Overs
- **Recipe Drawer**: Pins to the right edge with a sharp, vertical `2px solid #2c2416` boundary and zero corner softening. Steps are presented as a numbered checklist with high-contrast ingredient breakdowns.
