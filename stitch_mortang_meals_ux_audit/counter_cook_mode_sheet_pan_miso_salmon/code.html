Step 3: Arrange & Broil Salmon with Bok Choy

soup_kitchen
Target Glaze Caramelization Deep golden lacquer with slight bubbling char on bok choy tips
Visual Check

1. Lay the seasoned wild salmon fillets skin-side down onto the lined baking sheet, spacing them at least 2 inches apart to allow hot air circulation.

2. Arrange the halved bok choy cut-side up around the perimeter of the pan. Drizzle the bok choy with 1 tbsp sesame oil and lightly sprinkle with salt.

3. Spoon and brush the miso-tamari glaze generously across the top of each fillet, ensuring full surface coverage.

4. Transfer to the top third of your oven. Broil on High for 10 to 12 minutes until the miso glaze begins bubbling into a rich mahogany char and the salmon flakes with gentle fork pressure.

Counter Pro-Tip: Perfect Bok Choy Texture

If your broiler runs exceptionally hot, tent a loose strip of foil over the leafy green bok choy tips at the 7-minute mark to prevent scorched greens while the salmon caramelizes.
