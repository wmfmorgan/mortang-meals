Slow-Cooker Coconut Lentil Curry

⏱ 30 min prep · 🔥 Low simmer 4 hrs · Serves 4
Step 1: Aromatics

Dice 1 yellow onion, mince 4 cloves garlic, grate 2 tbsp fresh ginger. Heat 1 tbsp coconut oil in skillet until fragrant (2m).

Step 2: Dry Spice Toasting

Add 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp garam masala. Stir continuously for 45 seconds until deeply toasted.

Step 3: Slow Cooker Load

Transfer aromatics to slow cooker. Add 2 cups rinsed red lentils, 1 can crushed tomatoes, 1 can full-fat coconut milk, and 3 cups vegetable broth.

Allergy Verification Flag

Ensure vegetable stock brand is certified peanut-free and gluten-free (Leo & Maya compatibility).
